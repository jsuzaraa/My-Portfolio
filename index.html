How a project runs

  1. 01

    Understand

    I start by understanding the project, its goals, requirements, and what is expected. I make sure I have a clear idea of what needs to be done before getting started.

  2. 02

    Plan

    I organize the tasks, identify the tools and resources I need, and create a simple plan to keep the project structured and on track.

  3. 03

    Learn & Build

    I work on the project while learning any tools, processes, or skills that I may not be familiar with. I'm comfortable asking questions, researching solutions, and adapting when needed.

  4. 04

    Review

    I carefully review my work, check for errors, and make improvements based on the requirements or feedback.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Once everything is completed and reviewed, I prepare the final output and make sure it is organized, functional, and ready to use.

  6. 06

    Import

    I take feedback as an opportunity to learn. Every project is a chance to improve my skills, discover better ways of working, and take on new challenges.
